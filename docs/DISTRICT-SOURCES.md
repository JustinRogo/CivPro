# Nationwide district civil-rules source catalog

Research date: **September 6, 2026**. Covers all **94 federal district and territorial courts**. The [U.S. Courts directory definition](https://www.uscourts.gov/federal-court-finder-help-definitions) supplies the coverage count; the [national rules page](https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure) directs users to individual courts for local rules.

**93 courts have an official PDF source located; 92 have a primary PDF listed without the known future-edition issue below.** Across shared rulebooks and two general-rules companions, all **94 distinct PDF URLs** were downloaded and verified as PDF bytes. All files were opened with a PDF parser and their first two pages checked for identification (the replacement Southern Georgia PDF was separately opened). SHA-256 hashes, byte counts, page counts, resolved URLs, and retrieval timestamps are in the [machine-readable catalog](../sources/district-catalog.json).

This inventory covers the main district-wide civil rules, sometimes inside a broader rulebook. It is not an exhaustive inventory of standing orders, individual judges' practices, specialty rules, or forms. Court pages can link older compilations supplemented by later orders. Every new source still needs legal-currency, amendment, and civil-content boundary review before ingestion. No new district has been added to the reader.

## Exceptions and onboarding notes

- **Oregon:** official civil rules and a complete printable HTML edition located; no current court-issued compiled civil PDF found. A browser-generated PDF would be a derivative.
- **Western Michigan:** the court labels its PDF the 2026 version and says amendments take effect 60 days after its August 13, 2026 order. Keep this PDF separate from rules effective on September 6; the prior civil edition is available as HTML.
- **Shared rules:** Eastern/Southern New York share one source URL. Arkansas, Iowa, Kentucky, and Mississippi have joint rulebooks hosted separately; do not assume their files are identical.
- **Separate general rules:** Guam and Nebraska general-rules PDFs are included alongside their civil PDFs. Other supporting collections need review during onboarding.
- **Southern Georgia:** replaced a broken printable-PDF link with an alternate official civil-rules PDF; adoption of the court's proposed 2026 revisions still needs checking.
- **Later amendments:** Puerto Rico and Northern West Virginia visibly list later amendment material separately from their compiled books. Review it before importing.
- **Western Wisconsin:** its court-listed local-rules PDF is only two pages; additional pretrial procedures require a separate inventory.

## Recheck and acquire

Downloaded PDFs are staged under `candidate/district-pdfs/` by SHA-256 filename. That directory is intentionally ignored by Git. The approved Connecticut/federal snapshots and canonical `data/` were not changed. To recreate the download set:

```sh
python scripts/verify-district-sources.py --catalog sources/district-catalog.json --output candidate/district-pdfs
```

The verifier writes `verification.json` with per-URL outcomes; inspect any failures. It never promotes files into approved snapshots or edits the catalog. A successful HTTP response without a `%PDF-` signature is rejected. The official Idaho packet is generated dynamically, so new capture bytes can differ even without a rule amendment.

To rediscover links for manual review:

```sh
python scripts/discover-district-sources.py --input sources/district-catalog.json --output candidate/district-discovery
```

Discovery is a bounded HTML-link crawler, not a currency checker. JavaScript pages (such as Hawaii), download endpoints, archives, pending changes, and separate amendments require manual review. Follow [district onboarding](ADDING-A-DISTRICT.md) and [ingestion maintenance](INGESTION.md) after choosing the next district.
